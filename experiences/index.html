Experiences

A safari is more than a drive.

Walk with a ranger, glide among hippos, cycle to the hot springs, then drive deep into the park. Every day is shaped with Leepa, at the pace you choose.

Walking safari

In the park · about 2 hours · with an armed ranger · on a game drive day

On foot, the bush changes completely. You learn to read tracks, smell the plants and notice the small life you miss from a car. Walking safaris take place during a full-day game drive.

US$ 65 per person, in addition to the game drive

Boat safari

In the park · on the water · on a game drive day

Glide among hippos, crocodiles and water birds, with the palms of the riverbank all around. Boat safaris take place during a full-day game drive.

US$ 95 per person, in addition to the game drive

Forest walk with Leepa

Around camp

Leepa shares what he learned as a Maasai: the roots, barks and leaves of traditional medicine, the tracks of small animals, the sounds of the forest.

US$ 25 per person

Bike to the hot springs

Outside the park · half day

A ride along red earth tracks to natural hot springs hidden in the forest, then back through Kisaki.

US$ 25 per person

Kisaki village

Outside the park

Meet the people who live beside the park and the daily rhythm of the last village before Nyerere. If your dates allow, we take you to the weekly market or to the pastoralists' market, where herders from the whole region gather.

US$ 25 per person

Full-day game drive

In the park · full day

A whole day in Nyerere in an open-roof 4x4, with breakfast and lunch in the bush. The western sector is quiet: you will often have elephants, giraffes or lions to yourselves.

From US$ 245 per person, park fees included (2 guests)

Sundowner

Around camp · late afternoon

A drink in hand as the sun sets over the bush and the hills turn gold.

US$ 25 per person

Wildlife

What you may see.

Elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras, buffalo, hippos, crocodiles, impalas, baboons and more than 440 bird species recorded in the park. Nothing is guaranteed in the wild: that is what makes every sighting special.
