How to get here, when to come, what to bring.

Getting here

Three ways to reach Lemara.

Recommended

SGR train to Ngerengere

The fast, modern electric train from Dar es Salaam. We pick you up at Ngerengere station and drive you to camp.

For adventurers

TAZARA train to Kisaki

The historic Tanzania–Zambia line stops in Kisaki itself, a few kilometres from camp. Slower and less predictable, unforgettable.

Short on time

Fly to Matambwe

Light aircraft land at Matambwe airstrip, a short drive from camp. We meet you on arrival.

When to come

Lemara is open all year.

Dry season

June to October

The classic safari season. The bush thins out, animals gather near water and game viewing is at its best.

Green season

November to March

Lush landscapes, newborn animals, migrant birds and very few visitors. Short showers, mostly in the afternoon.

Long rains

Late March to early May

Heavy rains. Some tracks can close. Quiet and green, for those who come for the camp, the village and the forest.

What to bring

Packing list.

  • Light clothes in neutral colours, long sleeves for the evening
  • Good walking shoes
  • A warm layer for early game drives
  • Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen
  • Insect repellent
  • Binoculars and a camera
  • A torch or headlamp

Questions

Before you travel.

Do I need a visa?

Most visitors need a visa for Tanzania. It can be arranged on arrival at the airport, or online in advance on the official e-visa website of the Tanzanian government.

What about health?

Ask your doctor or a travel clinic about malaria prevention and vaccinations, well before departure.

Is it safe to walk in the bush?

Walking safaris inside the park are always led by an armed park ranger. Around camp, Leepa and the team guide you.

Can you cater for my diet?

Yes: vegetarian, vegan, halal and dairy-free meals are prepared on request.

Is there electricity and Wi-Fi?

Solar power with a back-up generator, and free Wi-Fi at the bar.

How do I pay?

By credit card (a surcharge applies), by bank transfer or in cash. We confirm the payment terms with your booking.

Ready to come and meet us?

Tell us your dates and how you like to travel. Leepa or the team usually reply within 24 hours, unless they are out in the bush!
