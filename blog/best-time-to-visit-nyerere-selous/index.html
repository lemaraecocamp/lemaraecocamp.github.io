Best time to visit Nyerere (Selous), month by month

A practical guide from Leepa and the Lemara team.

← All articles

There is no bad time to visit Nyerere, only different ones. Lemara is open all year. Here is what each season brings, so you can choose the one that suits you.

At a glance

Months Season In one line
June to October Dry season The classic safari: animals gather around water, the bush thins out
November to March Green season Lush landscapes, newborn animals, migrant birds, very few visitors
Late March to early May Long rains Heavy rain, some tracks closed, the camp and the forest for yourselves

June to October: the dry season

The rains are over, the grass dries and the trees lose their leaves. Animals concentrate around rivers, lakes and waterholes, so wildlife is easier to find. Days are sunny and warm, mornings and evenings cooler.

  • Best for: first safaris, game viewing, walking safaris.
  • Good to know: it is the busiest time of year in the park, but the western side stays quiet. Book early.

November to March: the green season

Short rains return, usually as afternoon showers. The bush turns green, many animals give birth, and migrant birds arrive from Europe and the rest of Africa. Light is soft, skies are dramatic, and you may have whole areas of the park to yourself.

  • Best for: photographers, birdwatchers, travellers who love quiet.
  • Good to know: many parts of the park are not accessible in this season, and thicker vegetation can make animals harder to spot. Lemara, meanwhile, is at its most luxuriant. Some days can be hot and humid.

Late March to early May: the long rains

This is the wettest time of year. Heavy rain can make some tracks in the park impassable, and game drives depend on the conditions of the day.

  • Best for: travellers who come for the camp, the forest walks, Kisaki village and the sound of rain on a thatched roof.
  • Good to know: ask us before you book: we will tell you honestly what is possible.

Month by month

Month What to expect
January Green and lush, short showers, newborn animals, migrant birds
February Hot, green, quiet; a lovely month for birds
March Rains increase towards the end of the month
April Long rains: the wettest month
May Rains fade, landscapes at their greenest
June Start of the dry season; cool mornings
July Dry and pleasant; excellent game viewing
August Dry; animals gather near water
September Hot and dry; superb wildlife
October Hottest month; the bush is bare and animals easy to see
November Short rains begin; the land turns green again
December Green season; migrant birds; festive and quiet

Our tip

If you can travel in June or early November, you get the best of both worlds: good wildlife, comfortable temperatures and very few visitors.
