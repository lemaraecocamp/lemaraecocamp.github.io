How to get to Nyerere (Selous) by train

A practical guide from Leepa and the Lemara team.

← All articles

Most visitors fly into Nyerere National Park, the park formerly known as the Selous. But there is another way in, slower, greener and far more memorable: the train. Here is how it works, from Dar es Salaam to our camp at Lemara, on the quiet western side of the park.

Two railways, two moods

Two railway lines leave Dar es Salaam towards the west of Nyerere, and they could hardly be more different.

The SGR, fast and modern. Tanzania's new electric Standard Gauge Railway links Dar es Salaam and Morogoro, about 300 km inland. Its stations are Dar es Salaam, Pugu, Soga, Ruvu, Ngerengere and Morogoro. Ngerengere lies 135 km from Dar es Salaam, and it is where we meet our guests. The ordinary trains stop at every station; the express trains go straight to Morogoro without stopping, so make sure you book an ordinary train to Ngerengere.

The TAZARA, historic and wild. The Tanzania–Zambia Railway, opened in the 1970s, runs south-west from Dar es Salaam towards Zambia, straight through the bush. It stops in Kisaki itself, the village a few kilometres from Lemara. Cross-border passenger services resumed in February 2026 after a long pause. The train is slow, often several hours late, and unforgettable: villages, forests and sometimes wildlife roll past the window.

Option 1: SGR to Ngerengere (recommended)

  1. Book your ticket online on the official SGR ticketing site of Tanzania Railways Corporation, or at the station. Choose an ordinary train that stops at Ngerengere.
  2. Board at the SGR station in Dar es Salaam. The trains are clean, air-conditioned and punctual.
  3. Get off at Ngerengere. Our driver waits for you on the platform.
  4. Drive to Lemara through the countryside and villages (about 3h30). You arrive in time for a forest walk with Leepa or a sundowner.

The transfer from Ngerengere is included in all our stay packages (not in single-night bookings).

Option 2: TAZARA to Kisaki (for adventurers)

  1. Check the timetable with TAZARA or with us before you plan: services change, and delays of a few hours are normal.
  2. Board at the TAZARA station in Dar es Salaam, a different station from the SGR.
  3. Get off at Kisaki. We pick you up at the station, at any hour. One of our guests wrote: “There is absolutely nothing to fault with Lemara from the moment Leepa collected us from Kisaki train station.”

Bring snacks, water and patience. In return, you get one of the great train rides in Africa.

Why come by train?

  • It is part of the journey. You see the real Tanzania, not just the clouds.
  • It is lighter on the planet than a light aircraft.
  • It costs less than a charter flight, which leaves more for your days in the park.
  • It takes you to the quiet side. Most flights land in the east of the park. The train brings you to the west, where few vehicles go.

Practical tips

  • Keep your passport at hand: it is needed to buy tickets.
  • Travel light, with a soft bag: easier on trains and in safari vehicles.
  • Tell us your train before you travel, so we can meet you.
  • Combine with the beach: take the ferry from Zanzibar to Dar es Salaam, then the train.
