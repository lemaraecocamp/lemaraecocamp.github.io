Stays

Stay three nights or more.

That is when Lemara truly becomes yours. Every stay is shaped with Leepa, and can be adapted to you.

Always included

  • Transfer from Ngerengere SGR station (or Kisaki TAZARA station)
  • Full board, home-cooked Tanzanian food
  • All activities listed, with Leepa or our guides
  • Park fees for game drives, walking and boat safaris
  • Drinking water and Wi-Fi at the bar

Not included

  • Train tickets and flights
  • Drinks from the bar
  • Tips and travel insurance

Good to know

Prices are per person, based on two guests sharing a banda. Solo travellers, families and groups: ask us for a quote. Longer stays get the best value.
